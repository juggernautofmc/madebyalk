ABOUT ME
Hi! I'm Sami Alkharrat


I'm 18 years old, I grew up in Los Gatos, California, and went to Los Gatos High School, where I discovered my passion for graphic design. Now, I am a professional graphic designer based in the Twin Cities where I am also pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science at the University of Minnesota.

I'm also a professional martial artist competing on the NASKA circuit for the past two years. I started martial arts back in 2018 and started graphic design in 2022, and I decided to merge these two passions to hopefully give back to the martial arts community and to provide school owners with a way to make their brand as presentable and professional as possible! However, I still take on plenty of design jobs for other small business owners as well!
