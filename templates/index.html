ABOUT ME
Hi! I'm Sami Alkharrat


I'm an 18-year-old graphic designer and professional martial artist from Los Gatos, California, now based in the Twin Cities. I discovered my passion for graphic design while attending Los Gatos High School and am currently pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science at the University of Minnesota.

I've been training in martial arts since 2018 and have competed on the NASKA circuit for the past two years. In 2022, I started my journey in graphic design and soon realized I could merge both passions to give back to the martial arts community. My goal is to help school owners strengthen their brand with clean, visually appealing designs that make a lasting impression. I also work with small business owners beyond the martial arts industry, creating designs that help their brands stand out.
